# lumenOS

an entire operating system that lives inside a browser tab. one html file. that's it. my aim of having my own os, combined by my interest in the way apple designs its own websites and os, has made this possible.

view-source it. i dare you.

## why did i build this

mainly because i could. also "how hard can an os be, really" (answer: very, but the fun kind of very). and i wanted to understand every single line of what i ship — if a window drags, that's MY code dragging it. no libraries taking the credit.

also i get to say "i built an operating system" at age when other people are still stuck on todo apps.

## the apps (all bangers)

- **windows** — drag em, resize em, minimize em, yeet em (close). IT'S JUST LIKE APPLE'S WEBSITE
- **dock** — mac hover magnify + little dots for running apps. extremely cute, extremely essential. which is just so so elegant and smooth
- **dynamic island** — yes i cloned the iphone thing, only cuz i love the way it looks. it morphs between an equalizer, notifications and a music player with one bouncy css transition.
- **terminal** — `help`, `neofetch`, `echo`, `open music`. can't rm -rf your homework though, sorry. the neofetch exists purely because every operating system is legally required to have a neofetch.
- **music** — there are no mp3s. the beats are generated by MATH, live, with WebAudio. triangle chords, sine bass, pentatonic sprinkles and fake vinyl hiss. no spotify was harmed.
- **notes** — autosaves as you type. dopamine included free of charge.
- **settings** — wallpapers, accent colors, factory reset for when you want to pretend it's a fresh start.
- **deep links** — `index.html#open=terminal,music` boots straight into those apps. screenshot speedrun tech.

## PROBLEMS I FACED!!

- the ENTIRE os went blank because one neofetch line ended with `;` instead of `,`. one (1) character. the console said nothing useful, as is tradition.
- windows used to jitter while dragging until i tracked the offset between cursor and window corner instead of the cursor itself. physics? no. just vibes and math.

stability report: more stable than my sleep schedule.
